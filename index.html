Privacy Policy – Endorsement Maker

Last updated: 25 November 2026
Developer: VA-GR
Contact: kachakra1997@gmail.com

Endorsement Maker is a desktop app that adds text blocks ("endorsements") to PDF pages and saves a new PDF.

What the app does with your data

  • Everything happens on your computer. The app does not connect to the internet, has no user accounts, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • PDF files you open are read only to show and edit them. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • Templates you create (text, recipients, signature, layout) are saved locally in the app's own storage on your computer.
  • When you export a PDF or a template file, it is written only to the location you choose in the Save dialog.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, share or sell personal information.
  • It does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Your control

Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored templates. Files you exported remain where you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
